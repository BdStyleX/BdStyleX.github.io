Hisab Khata – Privacy Policy

Hisab Khata is a personal income and expense tracker. Last updated: October 2026.

What we store. The name, mobile number or email you enter, a hashed password, and the entries you add (amounts, categories, reasons, dates). All of this is stored only on your own device, in the app’s local storage.

What we do not do. We do not send your data to any server, sell it, share it with third parties, or use advertising or analytics trackers. The app loads a web font from Google Fonts.

Deleting your data. Clear the app’s storage in your phone settings or uninstall the app, and all data is removed.
