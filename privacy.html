Hisab Khata – Privacy Policy

Hisab Khata is a personal income and expense tracker. Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

Your name and email address, your password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it in readable form), and the entries you add: amounts, categories, reasons, dates, and your chosen currency.

Where it is stored

On your device, and on Google Firebase servers (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) linked to your account so that you can restore your records on a new phone. Data is encrypted in transit. Only you can read your records; other users cannot.

How we use it

Only to run the app: sign-in, saving, syncing and restoring your records. We do not show ads, do not sell your data, and do not share it with anyone except Google Firebase, which processes it on our behalf.

Deleting your data

Open the app, go to the bottom of the main screen and tap “Delete account”. This permanently deletes your account and all of your records from our servers. You can also write to us at the address below and we will delete it for you.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.
