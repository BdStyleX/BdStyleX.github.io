FULL DESCRIPTION:
Hisab Khata is a simple income and expense tracker made for people living abroad.

Write down every amount with its reason, see your total balance at a glance, and check a month-by-month summary. Every category (rent, food, salary, overtime, money sent home and more) has its own dashboard with totals, monthly average, a 6-month chart and the full list of entries.

Features
- Total balance, monthly income and monthly expenses
- A separate dashboard for each category
- Works in English, Bangla, Arabic, Hindi and Malay
- 14 currencies, including BDT, SAR, AED, QAR, KWD, OMR, MYR, SGD, USD, GBP, EUR, INR
- Save and restore a backup file
- Light and dark mode, works offline

Your data stays on your phone. No ads.
